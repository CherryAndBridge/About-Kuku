What information does the Application obtain and how is it used?

The Application does not require registration and has no user accounts. It does not collect, log or store personal information on the Service Provider's systems during normal use. Your settings, chime schedule and saved places are stored only on your device.

The Service Provider receives information in two cases only.

Feedback: you can choose to send feedback from the Application's settings. A feedback message contains the feedback type you pick (bug or improvement) and the text you write. Nothing else is attached: no name, email address, device identifier or location. Because you write the text yourself, please do not include personal details you do not want to share.

Premium purchases: if you buy the premium version, Google Play gives the Service Provider the order details (such as order number, product, price, date and country) and lets it look up an order by the buyer's email address. The Service Provider uses these only to handle the purchase, refunds and support, and to meet accounting and tax obligations. It never receives your payment details.

The Service Provider does not send marketing communications.

Does the Application collect precise real time location information of the device?

With your permission, the Application accesses your device's precise location, including in the background, for one purpose: to stay silent inside the places you set (or, if you choose, to chime only inside them).

  • Your location and saved places are processed on your device and are never sent to the Service Provider. When you add a place or search for an address, Google receives the information described under Google Maps and Google Places below.
  • Checking whether you are inside a place uses the location and geofencing services of Google Play Services, which Google processes under its own privacy policy.
  • Saved places are excluded from Android's cloud backup, so they do not leave your device through backups.
  • You can withdraw location access at any time in your device's settings. The Application keeps working, but location-based silencing stops.

What are your GDPR data protection rights?

Under the GDPR, you have the following rights:

  • Right of Access: You can request access to your personal data.
  • Right to Rectification: You can request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Right to Erasure: You can request deletion of your personal data (the "right to be forgotten").
  • Right to Restrict Processing: You can request that the Data Controller limits how they use your data.
  • Right to Data Portability: You can request a copy of your data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format.
  • Right to Object: You can object to processing based on legitimate interests, including the processing of feedback.

Because feedback is not linked to your identity, please identify the message concerned (its text, or roughly when you sent it) when exercising these rights.

If you believe your data protection rights have been violated, you have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Contact details for each country's Data Protection Authority can be found at: https://edpb.ec.europa.eu/about-edpb/members_en
